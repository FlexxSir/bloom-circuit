The goal

Rotate the paths until every tile connects to the fixed golden seed. A tile glows when it receives light. Flowers bloom when their paths connect.

No countdown. The clock pauses when you pause or leave the tab.

The controls

  • Click or tap: rotate clockwise.
  • Right-click or Shift-click: rotate counterclockwise.
  • Keyboard: arrows to explore, Enter or Space to turn.
  • Undo restores a move and counts as another turn.
  • Hint fixes and locks a tile for a 180-point penalty.
